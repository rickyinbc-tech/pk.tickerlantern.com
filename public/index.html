Equity assumes every opponent holds a hand from the range you chose and that the hand reaches showdown. Real opponents narrow their ranges with every action, and future betting, fold equity, rake, side pots, tournament payouts and player tendencies can change the decision. The suggestions are rules of thumb built on these numbers—not solver output and not a guarantee of the best action.
